Verify the Law

Σ I_in = Σ I_out
The junction is balanced: the total incoming current equals the total outgoing current.

You can deliberately unbalance the junction by changing one of the currents, then observe the difference.

Verify Kirchhoff’s Second Law

In a closed loop, the algebraic sum of the electromotive forces equals the algebraic sum of the potential differences across the resistors.
